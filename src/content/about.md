I studied Computer Programming and Analysis at Algonquin College. My work has taken me through software, data, and the small improvements that make everyday systems easier to use.

This is a place for that work, and for the curiosity around it. Some things are finished. Some are still taking shape.

_RIKT_ means empty, or void. Here, it is an open centre: room for the things I learn, make, and give meaning to.
